## Our edge, measured

- **Moment choice:** Trailer 2 02:20–02:47, scored on the clipper checklist:
  stands alone (no context needed — pure action), hooks immediately (jet ski
  → Jason on the bike in the first 2 s), payoff inside the clip (boat chase,
  then the GTA VI logo), one idea (the most insane 30 seconds), fits
  vertical framing (action stays centre-frame after the letterbox crop).
- **Full-bleed vertical:** Trailer 2 is 2.39:1 letterboxed inside 1080p
  (content rows 136–944). A naive 9:16 crop keeps the black bars
  (verified: first render had 192 px bars top and bottom). We crop the
  letterbox out first, then centre-crop to 9:16 — no black bars, figures
  whole in frame (verified at t=1, 2, 5, 12, 26, 30).
- **Clean captions, not raw auto-subs:** YouTube's auto-subs for Trailer 2
  are rolling/duplicated cues. We burned exactly one cue — the single spoken
  line inside the window ("Hey, if you got friends, can you hook me up,
  please?", source 02:30–02:37, clip 10.0–17.3 s) — each cue showing only
  the words spoken in its time range. Verified frame-by-frame against the
  speech.
- **make.fun pitch:** 5 s end card over the GTA VI logo frame, on screen
  long enough to read: "Make clips like this with AI / and earn money on
  make.fun / make.fun/bounty/gta6-clipper-01". The brief requires the pitch
  in the clip; the description carries the $500-bounty line + play code.

## Tried and dropped

- A 115–145 s window (explosion + car crash + bike) — rejected: the
  explosion shot is dark and the payoff (bike) lands mid-clip with a weak
  ending; the 140–167 s window ends on the VI logo, a natural payoff.
- Raw YouTube auto-subs burned as-is — rejected: rolling cues repeat the
  previous line and lag the speech (standing rule: never burn raw auto-subs).
- Naive 9:16 crop of the full 1080 frame — rejected: kept the trailer's
  letterbox bars (verified 192 px bars); re-rendered with the letterbox
  cropped first.

## Expected outcome

Views decide everything; likes/comments/length/title do not. With the race
~1 h old and #1 at 208 views, an early public Short with a top-10-pattern
title has a realistic shot at a paid place (top 20). The main risk is
distribution, not the clip: if v1's live views lag after ~24 h, a v2 with a
different moment (Lucia prison walkout, 00:50–01:20) is the next move.
